## Full description (4000 characters max)

Breeq is a brick breaker built by players, for players.

Every wall is a photo someone chose, covered in glowing glass bricks and bonus
zones that bend the ball's speed. Take the paddle, clear the wall, and the next
one is waiting.

PLAY THE STORY
Follow Kal and the Lanterns across three seasons of hand-built episodes. Each
clear pays XP, the level bar climbs, and the road opens further.

BUILD YOUR OWN WALL
The editor is the game: place bricks over your own photo or one of twenty skies,
drop ½, ×2 and ×3 bonus rings, and a robot proves your wall can be cleared before
anyone pays to try it. Every published wall gets a difficulty rating, from Gentle
to Brutal.

PLAY TO EARN
From level 5, the Earn store opens. Players publish walls with a pot in ETH; you
pay a ticket in gems to try one, and a clear pays the pot to your balance, once
per wall. Publish your own walls and set the price. Withdraw when you want.

MADE FOR THE PHONE
Portrait only, one thumb, a canvas that fills the screen. Sound designed for
each level's key, haptics on every hit, and nothing that needs a subscription.

INVITE FRIENDS
Share a cleared wall on X, WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook: friends who sign up
through your link earn you gems.

Breeq runs on the same account as breeq.space: sign in with Google or email and
your gems, ETH balance, XP and walls follow you.
